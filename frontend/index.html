A live ETA, built by the people on the bus

Most local buses have no tracker and no timetable you can trust. When a commuter sees a bus and taps “I see the bus”, we use that sighting and the usual travel time between stops to tell everyone further down the route how long they have to wait.

Waiting without knowing

90 minutes

I’ve waited about 90 minutes at my stop on the way home from college, more than once. No board, no app, no one to ask.

Why TransitPulse exists

  • No information at the stop

    You only find out the bus is late by waiting for it.

  • You can’t plan your time

    Leave too early and you stand around. Leave late and you miss it.

  • Did it already pass?

    Without any signal, every minute of waiting is a guess.

Three taps, no guessing

  1. 1

    Pick your route and stop

    Choose the route, the direction the bus is going, and the stop you’re waiting at. Star it to find it in one tap next time.

  2. 2

    Check the live ETA

    See a countdown to the bus, worked out from the latest sighting further up the route and the average travel time between stops.

  3. 3

    Spot the bus? Share it

    Tap “I see the bus” and add the operator and how crowded it is. Everyone waiting ahead of you gets a better ETA, and you earn badges.

Sightings older than 30 minutes are marked stale instead of guessed.
